# Upskill-Campaign-Performance-Dashboard
This project analyzes the performance of the Upskill advertising campaign using Power BI.

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the performance of the Upskill advertising campaign using Power BI. The objective was to evaluate campaign effectiveness, measure lead generation performance, identify the best-performing ad creatives, and assess the overall return on investment (ROI).
The dashboard tracks the customer journey from ad impressions to lead generation and provides insights into campaign efficiency and conversion performance.

## Business Objectives
The analysis was designed to answer the following questions:
* How many people were reached by the campaign?
* How many users visited the landing page?
* How many leads were generated?
* What was the cost of acquiring each lead?
* Which ad creative generated the most leads?
* How effectively did landing page visitors convert into leads?
* What return was generated from the advertising investment?

## Key Metrics Calculated
### Click-Through Rate (CTR)
Measures the percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks. 
CTR = Link Clicks / Impressions * 100

### Landing Page Conversion Rate
Measures the percentage of clicks that resulted in a landing page visit. 
Landing Page Conversion Rate = Landing Page Views / Link Clicks * 100

### Link Clicks × 100
Measures the average cost of acquiring a lead.
Cost Per Lead = Amount Spent / Total Leads

### Student Conversion Rate
Paid Students / Total Leads × 100

### Return on Investment (ROI)
Measures the revenue generated relative to advertising spend.
ROI = Revenue / Ad Spend

## Key Insights
* The campaign generated 49 leads from 387 landing page visits.
* Landing page conversion rate reached 12.66%.
* Cost per lead averaged ₦247.35.
* CTR indicated moderate engagement with campaign creatives.
* Children's video creatives generated the majority of leads.
* ROI reached 4.3x, meaning every ₦1 spent generated approximately ₦4.30 in revenue.
* Only a small percentage of leads converted into paying students, highlighting an opportunity to improve lead nurturing and follow-up processes.

## Recommendations
* Increase budget allocation toward children's video creatives due to stronger lead generation performance.
* Improve lead nurturing through WhatsApp follow-ups and automated reminders.
* Add testimonials and social proof to the post-registration experience to improve student conversion rates.
* Monitor conversion performance by day of the week to identify optimal advertising periods.
* Continue testing creative variations to improve CTR and lead acquisition efficiency.
